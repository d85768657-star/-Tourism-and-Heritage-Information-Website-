North India - Historical Monuments

Taj Mahal, Agra

A famous white marble monument built by Shah Jahan. It is a symbol of love.

Red Fort, Delhi

A historic fort that represents India's rich history and architecture.

South India - Famous Destinations

Hampi, Karnataka

Hampi is famous for ancient temples, stone monuments and the Vijayanagara Empire.

Kerala

Kerala is known for green landscapes, backwaters, beaches and beautiful nature.

Indian Cultural Festivals

Diwali

The festival of lights, celebrated with lamps, decorations and sweets.

Holi

The festival of colours, celebrated with colours, music and happiness.

Famous Indian Food

Hyderabadi Biryani

A popular rice dish prepared with aromatic spices and traditional ingredients.

South Indian Dosa

A crispy dish usually served with sambar and coconut chutney.

Travel Tips

✔ Book hotels and tickets in advance.

✔ Carry water, identification and necessary medicines.

✔ Respect local culture and traditions.

✔ Keep historical places clean.

✔ Check weather conditions before travelling.
